Growing Plants on the Moon

Engaging students in biology and engineering through lunar plant-growth experiments, sensor systems, and hands-on design.

This site supports teachers interested in the Plant the Moon and Plant the Moon Jr. competitions, as well as teachers seeking new ways to bring engineering and biology together.
